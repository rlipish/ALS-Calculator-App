At present, there are four main criteria that continue to be used both in the clinical and research settings for the diagnosis of ALS. In addition, all three of these criteria have their differences and subtleties.  They contain rules that many clinicians and researchers may not be aware of. Because of this, errors can occur in classification especially with those who may not have extensive experience in the field. As a consequence, mistakes could result with respect to the diagnosis of ALS in the clinical setting, as well as incorrect decisions being made with respect to inclusion and exclusion criteria in research trials.  
We developed a computerized spreadsheet calculator that facilitates uniformity and accuracy in the classification of ALS based on the El Escorial (1), Airlie House (2), Awaji-Shima (3), and Gold Coast (4) Criteria.
